About this model

An interactive companion to a semi-analytical reservoir-routing model: a Williams–Hann design-flood hydrograph is routed through a reservoir shaped by the Mohammadzadah-Habili dimensionless depth–storage curve, through a combined spillway + orifice outlet — starting full or empty. Every chart recomputes live, numerically, in your browser.

Numerical routing uses a fixed-step RK4 integration of a storage-space reformulation (dS/dt = I(t) − O(h(S))) — smooth and non-singular even for an empty-starting reservoir, unlike integrating in depth-space directly. The full semi-analytical (closed-form) solution, its derivation, and its validation against this numerical benchmark are in the theory paper and Colab notebook.

Reference: Kamis, A.S., Bahrawi, J.A., Elfeki, A.M. (2018). Reservoir routing in ephemeral streams in arid regions. Arabian Journal of Geosciences, 11:106.

Related: Storm Hydrograph — a Raindrop's Journey to the Gauge (unit-hydrograph / φ-index / Nash-cascade simulator, separate repository).
